📜 Paytable

↔ TWO-WAY PAYS: Wins read left → right AND right → left on every payline. If a line of 3 matches in the middle, it pays both ways for double the reward.

👻 WILD (Phantom): Substitutes for every regular symbol. SCATTER (Spirit): 3+ anywhere triggers Free Spins — 3 → 8, 4 → 12, 5+ → 20 spins.

🔮 CURSED CELLS: After every losing spin, one random cell becomes cursed. On the next spin, cursed cells have a 45% chance to spawn as Wild. Up to 5 cursed cells at once. All cursed cells clear on any win.

❓ How To Play

1. Set your bet with the + / − buttons.

2. Press SUMMON or SPACE. Use AUTO for up to 25 spins.

↔ Two-Way Paylines

Unlike standard slots, wins count in both directions. A line that starts with 3 matching symbols on the left pays, AND a line that starts with 3 matching symbols on the right pays. If a line has matching symbols at both ends, both wins apply — the middle cells can belong to both.

🔮 Cursed Cells

Every losing spin adds a curse to a random cell (up to 5). On the next spin, each cursed cell has a 45% chance to become a Wild. This means streaks of bad luck gradually flip the odds in your favour. The moment you win, all curses are lifted.

👻 Ghost Multiplier

During Free Spins, a Ghost Multiplier starts at ×1 and grows by +1 on every free spin (up to ×10). Every win is boosted by the current Ghost Multiplier — so the longer the bonus runs, the bigger the wins.

🌫️ Ghost Reveal

Symbols appear as ghosts first — semi-transparent with purple smoke — then materialize one by one with a smoky puff. Cursed cells glow with a purple sigil behind them.
