📜 Paytable

CLUSTER: 4 or more identical Runes touching orthogonally (no diagonals) pay a small win. RUNNER: a cluster that spans the entire board from left ↔ right or top ↔ bottom pays massively. A cluster that spans both directions is a CROSS RUNNER — the biggest win in the game.

WILD substitutes for any regular Rune. SCATTER pays anywhere: 3 → 8 free spins, 4 → 12, 5+ → 20. RIFT transforms itself and its neighbours into the dominant symbol on the board.

❓ How To Play

1. Set your bet with the + / − buttons.

2. Press SPIN or SPACE. Use AUTO for up to 25 spins.

🔮 Cluster Pays

4+ identical Runes touching each other (up, down, left, right) form a cluster. Wilds count as any regular Rune. Small clusters pay a modest win — the real prizes are Runners.

🏃 Runners

A cluster that reaches from the left edge to the right edge (or top to bottom) is a Runner and pays ×3 extra. If it spans BOTH directions, it's a Cross Runner — that's a ×8 bonus on top of the base cluster.

🌀 Rift Portals

When a Rift symbol lands, it morphs itself and its 4 orthogonal neighbours into the most common symbol on the board. This can chain clusters together and create Runners out of thin air.

💥 Cascade

Winning clusters explode, symbols fall down, new ones rise from the top. Each cascade step raises the multiplier — chain deeper for bigger wins.

⚡ Free Spins

3 Scatters = 8 spins · 4 = 12 spins · 5+ = 20 spins. All wins are ×2 during Free Spins. Scatters retrigger for +5 more spins.
