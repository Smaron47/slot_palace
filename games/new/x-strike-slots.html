⚡ Paytable

Wins are formed on the two diagonals. Match 3 identical symbols (Wilds substitute) → payout × bet.

⚡ FULL X BONUS: All five X positions [0,2,4,6,8] matching → pays both diagonals PLUS a 100× bet bonus.
👑 CORNER STRIKE: All 4 corners [0,2,6,8] matching → 30× bet bonus.
🌠 WILD: Substitutes for any regular symbol. When a Wild participates in a win, that win gets a ×2 boost (once per line).
✨ SCATTER: 3 anywhere → 8 Free Spins · 4 → 12 FS · 5+ → 20 FS. FS wins are ×2.

⚡ How To Play

1. Set your bet with the + / − buttons.

2. Press STRIKE or SPACE to spin the 3×3 board.

3. Wins form on either diagonal (top-left → bottom-right, or bottom-left → top-right).

4. Full X fires when all 5 cells of the X match — pays both diagonals + a big bonus.

5. Corner Strike fires when all 4 corners match — bonus payout.

6. Wilds substitute for any regular symbol and boost the win ×2 (once per line).

7. Scatters trigger Free Spins — 3/4/5+ → 8/12/20 spins, ×2 multiplier, retriggers add +5.
