📜 Paytable

MEGAWAYS: Every spin reels can have 2 to 7 rows — the total number of ways is the product of all reel heights. Matching symbols on 3+ consecutive reels from reel 1 pay out, and each additional copy of the symbol on a reel multiplies the ways.

WILD substitutes for every regular symbol. It never lands on reel 1.
SCATTER pays anywhere: 3+ scatters trigger FREE SPINS with a ×3 multiplier and reel height boost (reels 2-6 always start at 5+ rows).

❓ How To Play

1. Set your bet with the + / − buttons.

2. Press SPIN or SPACE. Use AUTO for up to 25 spins.

🌪️ Megaways

Each spin, reels 1-6 get a random number of rows (2-7). The more rows, the more ways. Maximum is 7×7×7×7×7×7 = 117,649 ways. Watch the WAYS badge roll up at the start of each spin.

💮 Ways Wins

A symbol wins if it lands on reel 1, 2, 3, and so on without a gap. Every extra copy on a reel multiplies the ways. If reel 1 has 2 cherry symbols, reel 2 has 3, and reel 3 has 2 — that's 2×3×2 = 12 ways of 3 cherries.

🌸 Wild

Wild never appears on reel 1, but it substitutes for every regular symbol everywhere else. A full-wild line pays as its own symbol.

⚡ Free Spins

3 Scatters = 8 free spins · 4 = 12 · 5+ = 20. During Free Spins all wins are multiplied ×3, and reels 2-6 are guaranteed to start with at least 5 rows — no mini-boards. Scatters retrigger for +5 spins.

🌊 Waterfall Animation

Symbols fall from above with magnetic deceleration — they enter fast, then slow down and lock in top-to-bottom, reel by reel, like a waterfall settling into a pool.
