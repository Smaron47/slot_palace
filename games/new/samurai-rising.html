❀ 1024 Ways

A 5×4 board has 4×4×4×4×4 = 1024 possible ways for a symbol to line up across consecutive reels. To win:

1. A symbol (or Wild) must appear on reel 1.

2. It must also appear on reel 2, then reel 3, and so on — with no gaps.

3. The number of ways = (count on reel 1) × (count on reel 2) × ...

4. You need at least 3 consecutive reels to win.

5. Payout = paytable × ways × bet, then multiplied by the current Chain Multiplier during a tumble.
