⚔ How To Play

1. Set your total bet with the + / − buttons or the MIN / STD / MAX presets. All 20 paylines are always active.

2. Press RAID or SPACE to spin the 5 reels.

3. Wins form when 3 or more matching symbols land on the same payline, from reel 1 leftwards.

4. Wilds substitute for any regular symbol. Every reel that contains a wild expands to fill all 3 rows.

5. Sunwheel Scatters trigger Free Spins — 3/4/5 → 10/15/20 spins, ×2 multiplier, retriggers add +5.

6. Choose your auto-spin count (10/25/50/100) and press AUTO. Press STOP to cancel.
