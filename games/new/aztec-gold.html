    ☼ How To Play

    1. Set your bet with the + / − buttons or the MIN / STD / MAX presets.

    2. Press SPIN or SPACE to spin the 5 reels.

    3. Regular wins are paid on 10 paylines (3+ matching symbols from reel 1).

    4. HOLD & WIN: When 5+ golden coins land, they lock in place and you get 3 respins. Every new coin that appears resets the counter to 3. When respins end, all coin values are summed and paid.
