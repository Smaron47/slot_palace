🏴‍☠️ Paytable

Wins form on the two diagonals. Match 3 identical symbols (Wilds substitute) → payout × bet.

⚔️ CROSS BONUS: All five X positions [0,2,4,6,8] matching → pays both diagonals PLUS a 100× bet bonus.
👑 CORNER HAUL: All 4 corners [0,2,6,8] matching → 30× bet bonus.
💀 SKULL WILD: Substitutes for any regular symbol. When a Wild participates in a win, that win gets a ×2 boost (once per line).
🌊 KRAKEN SCATTER: 3 anywhere → 8 Free Spins · 4 → 12 FS · 5+ → 20 FS. FS wins are ×2.

🏴‍☠️ How To Play

1. Set your bet with the + / − buttons.

2. Press SAIL or SPACE to spin the 3×3 board.

3. Wins form on either diagonal (top-left → bottom-right, or bottom-left → top-right).

4. Cross Bonus fires when all 5 cells of the X match — pays both diagonals + a big bonus.

5. Corner Haul fires when all 4 corners match — bonus payout.

6. Skull Wilds substitute for any regular symbol and boost the win ×2 (once per line).

7. Kraken Scatters trigger Free Spins — 3/4/5+ → 8/12/20 spins, ×2 multiplier, retriggers add +5.
