🌊 1×3 Layout

Unlike a traditional slot, this game has just one reel with three stacked symbols. It's compact, fast, and every spin matters. The reel scrolls downward — symbols sink from above like they're descending into the abyss — then settle with a bounce.

🐟 Winning

Match 3 of a kind for the full payout. Mid and high symbols also pay for 2 of a kind. Wilds (anglerfish) substitute for any regular symbol — including 2 wilds + 1 regular or 3 wilds (jackpot!).

💠 Dive Meter

Every losing spin adds +1 to the Dive Meter. When the meter reaches its target, the next spin is guaranteed to include at least one Wild. Meter resets after any win or after the wild spin.
