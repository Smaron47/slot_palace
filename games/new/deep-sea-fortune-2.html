🌊 How To Play

1. Set your total bet with the + / − buttons.

2. Press DIVE (or SPACE) to spin the reels. Each column spins bottom-to-top or top-to-bottom at random.

3. Symbols that form clusters of 5+ connected cells explode and pay out.

4. Remaining symbols fall down, new ones drop in from above, and any new clusters pay again — this is a cascade. Each cascade step boosts the tumble multiplier shown on the rolling multiplier reel.

5. Scatters (the shell) pay no cluster; instead 4+ on the board trigger FREE SPINS.

6. Press AUTO for 25 automatic dives, or STOP to cancel.
