📜 Paytable

Payouts are multiples of your line bet. Wins read left → right starting from reel 1. Three or more matching symbols required.

Wilds substitute for every regular symbol. Multiplier symbols (×2 / ×3) boost every winning line they sit on (default stacking: multiply, so ×2 + ×3 = ×6). Scatter pays anywhere and triggers Free Spins.

❓ How To Play

1. Choose your bet per line.

2. Choose active paylines. Total bet = line bet × active lines.

3. Press SPIN or SPACE. AUTO runs 25 spins; STOP cancels.

🌪️ Wild

Wilds replace any regular symbol but never replace Scatter or Multiplier symbols.

✖️ Multipliers

×2 and ×3 boost every winning payline they sit on. Default multiply stacking.

🔒 Sticky Wilds

Lock a cell as Wild for the rest of the Free Spin session.

↔️ Expanding Wilds

Turn a whole reel Wild for the spin (or whole session if configured).

🎁 Free Spins
