📜 Paytable

CLUSTER PAYS: Symbols win when 5 or more identical symbols touch each other on the 6×6 grid (up, down, left or right). Bigger clusters pay exponentially more. Clusters explode and new symbols fall in — cascades chain and multiply your win.

WILD counts as any regular symbol in a cluster. SCATTER pays anywhere: 3 → 8 free spins, 4 → 12, 5+ → 20. All free-spin wins are ×2.

❓ How To Play

1. Set your bet with the + / − buttons.

2. Press SPIN or SPACE. Use AUTO for up to 25 spins.

🔷 Cluster Pays

No paylines. A cluster is 5 or more matching symbols that touch each other (top, bottom, left, right — never diagonally). Wilds count as any symbol. Cluster payouts scale steeply with size.

💥 Cascade

After every cluster wins, its symbols explode. Symbols above fall down, new symbols drop in from the top, and the board is re-evaluated. Each cascade step raises the multiplier — up to ×100 on a deep chain.

🧭 Roaming Wild

A special Wild lives on the board and walks one cell per spin — up, down, left or right. It stays on the board during the whole cascade chain (it can't be destroyed). The purple badge shows you it's always active.

⚡ Free Spins

3 Scatters = 8 spins, 4 = 12 spins, 5+ = 20 spins. All wins ×2 during free spins. Scatters can retrigger for +5 more spins.
