📜 Paytable

Match 3+ identical symbols from left to right on an active payline. Payouts shown are multiples of your line bet.

1. Choose your bet per line using the + / − buttons.

2. Toggle active paylines or press ALL to activate every line.

3. Press SPIN or the SPACE key to play. Total bet = line bet × active paylines.

4. Use AUTO to spin automatically (up to 25 spins). Press STOP to cancel.
