🐉 How To Play

1. Set your total bet with the + / − buttons.

2. Press SPIN (or SPACE) to spin the reels. Columns scroll from top to bottom.

3. Symbols forming clusters of 4+ connected cells dissolve and pay out.

4. Remaining symbols fall down, new ones descend from above, and any new clusters pay again — this is the Jade Cascade. The Chain Multiplier reel at the top increases each step.

5. Dragon Wilds substitute for any regular symbol. Temple Scatters trigger Free Spins.

6. During Free Spins every win is multiplied ×3 and no bet is deducted.

7. Press AUTO for 25 automatic spins, or STOP to cancel.
