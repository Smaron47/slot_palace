💎 Base Game

5 paylines with 3+ matching symbols from left to right. Wild substitutes for every regular symbol. Cash Coins and Scatters don't form line wins.

💰 Hold & Win Bonus

When 6 or more Cash Coins land anywhere on the board, the bonus triggers:

• Every Cash Coin locks in place with its credit value.
• You get 3 respins. Every new coin that lands resets the counter to 3.
• Respins continue until the counter runs out or the board is full.
• All coin values are then added to your balance.

⭐ Grand Jackpot

If the entire 15-cell board fills with locked Cash Coins, you win the GRAND JACKPOT — a fixed 1000× your bet on top of all coin values.
