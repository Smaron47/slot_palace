📜 Paytable

WAYS TO WIN: Match a symbol on 3 or more consecutive reels starting from reel 1. Every extra copy of that symbol on a reel multiplies the ways. Total win = base pay × ways × bet.

WILD substitutes for every regular symbol. It also pays on its own.
SCATTER pays anywhere: 3 → 8 free spins, 4 → 12, 5+ → 20. Wins during free spins are multiplied ×2.

❓ How To Play

1. Set your bet with the + / − buttons.

2. Press SPIN or SPACE. Use AUTO for up to 25 spins.

🌌 Ways To Win

Unlike fixed paylines, every cell counts. If a symbol (or a Wild) appears on reel 1, 2 and 3, that's a 3-of-a-kind win — and every extra copy of the symbol on those reels multiplies the ways. 2×2×1 = 4 ways.

💥 Cascade

After every win the winning symbols explode. The remaining symbols fall down and new symbols rise up to fill the gaps. The cascade multiplier grows each step (×1 → ×2 → ×3 → ×5 → ×8 → ×10) and applies to the next win.

⚡ Free Spins

3 Scatters = 8 free spins, 4 = 12 spins, 5+ = 20 spins. All wins during free spins are ×2. Scatters can retrigger for +5 more spins.

🌪️ Wild

Wilds substitute for any regular symbol and also pay as their own symbol when 3+ appear on consecutive reels.
