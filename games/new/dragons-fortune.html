🔥 TOP BONUS REEL

Above the main grid sits a 5-cell bonus reel. Each cell shows a multiplier that applies only to the wins coming through that reel. A line win is split evenly across its matching reels, and each reel's share is multiplied by the value above it.

Example: A line pays 100 with 5 matching reels. Each reel contributes 20. If the top reel shows ×2 ×3 ×1 ×5 ×10, the win becomes 20×2 + 20×3 + 20×1 + 20×5 + 20×10 = 420.

🐉 How To Play

1. Set your bet with + / − or MIN / STD / MAX.

2. Press SPIN or SPACE. Both the main grid and the top bonus reel spin.

3. Wins form on 20 paylines — 3+ matching symbols from reel 1.

4. Each winning line's payout is split across its matching reels and each reel's share is multiplied by the bonus reel value above it.

5. Dragon Wilds substitute for any symbol.

6. Lantern Scatters trigger Free Spins (10/15/20) with retrigger +5.
