🌪️ Unique Reel System

Reels spin vertically, and each reel alternates direction — reel 1 tumbles up, reel 2 tumbles down, reel 3 tumbles up, and so on. Symbols also rotate a full 360° while they move, and the reel stretches vertically during fast spin.

⚡ Reel Multipliers

After all reels land, there's a chance that a multiplier badge drops onto a reel. Any payline that crosses that reel gets boosted by the multiplier (×2, ×3, ×5 or ×10). Multiple badges on different reels multiply together, so a ×5 on reel 2 and a ×3 on reel 4 gives ×15 on any win passing through both.
