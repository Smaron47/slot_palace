🔥 Paytable

This game has NO PAYLINES. Symbols pay simply for appearing anywhere on the 6×5 grid. Each symbol's payout depends on how many of it appear.

🔮 MYSTERY REVEAL: 3–5 random cells turn into "?" symbols before the spin. When the spin lands, all mystery cells reveal as the same symbol — potentially adding to a big count.
🔥 CASCADE: When a symbol type reaches 8+ occurrences, those cells explode, new symbols drop, and the Cascade Multiplier grows ×1 → ×2 → ×3 → ×5 → ×8 → ×12 → ×20 → ×30 → ×50 → ×100.
☀ SUN SCATTER: 4+ suns anywhere → 10 Free Spins · 5 → 15 · 6+ → 20. During Free Spins all wins are ×2 and every spin has 8–12 mystery cells!

🔥 Count Tiers

Total count of a symbol on the 30-cell grid determines the tier multiplier. Final payout = symbol base × tier multiplier × (bet ÷ 100).

    🔥 How To Play

    1. Set your bet with + / − or MIN / STD / MAX.

    2. Press IGNITE or SPACE. The 6×5 board fills with symbols.

    3. 8 or more of the same symbol anywhere on the board = WIN. The more you have, the bigger the multiplier.

    4. Watch the mystery "?" cells — 3–5 per spin. They all reveal as the SAME symbol mid-spin, which may push you past the 8+ threshold.

    5. Any winning symbol explodes and the board cascades with the Cascade Multiplier growing each step.

    6. Sun scatters (4+ anywhere) trigger Free Spins — 4/5/6+ → 10/15/20 spins. All wins are ×2.

    7. During Free Spins, every spin has 8–12 mystery cells — much bigger chances!
