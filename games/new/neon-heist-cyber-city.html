▚ Paytable

Wins form on the two diagonals. Match 3 identical symbols (Glitch Wilds substitute) → payout × bet.

◆ VAULT BONUS: All five X positions [0,2,4,6,8] matching → pays both diagonals PLUS a 100× bet bonus.
◆ CORNER BREACH: All 4 corners [0,2,6,8] matching → 30× bet bonus.
◆ GLITCH WILD: Substitutes for any regular symbol. When a Wild participates in a win, that win gets a ×2 boost (once per line).
◆ VIRUS SCATTER: 3 anywhere → 8 Free Spins · 4 → 12 FS · 5+ → 20 FS. FS wins are ×2.
◆ OVERCLOCK METER: Fills on every spin. When 100% is reached, the next spin gets a ×3 multiplier on all wins, then resets.

▚ How To Play

1. Set your bet with the + / − buttons or the MIN / STD / MAX presets.

2. Pick your auto-spin count (10 / 25 / 50 / 100), then press AUTO. Or just press RUN.

3. Wins form on either diagonal (top-left → bottom-right, or bottom-left → top-right).

4. Vault Bonus fires when all 5 cells of the X match — pays both diagonals + a big bonus.

5. Corner Breach fires when all 4 corners match — bonus payout.

6. Glitch Wilds substitute for any regular symbol and boost the win ×2 (once per line).

7. Virus Scatters trigger Free Spins — 3/4/5+ → 8/12/20 spins, ×2 multiplier, retriggers add +5.

8. Overclock Meter fills on every spin (more on wins). At 100% the next spin's wins get a ×3 OVERCLOCK multiplier, then the meter resets.
